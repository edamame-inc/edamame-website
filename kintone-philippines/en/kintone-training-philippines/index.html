Common questions

Questions before your consultation

What does Kintone training cover?
Two tracks. App-builder training teaches the people who'll own Kintone to create and change apps — fields, forms, views, permissions, workflows, and notifications — until they're unaided. End-user enablement teaches everyday staff to enter, find, and act on records so the system genuinely replaces Excel and paper.
What languages is the training delivered in?
English, Filipino, and Japanese. Our Japanese-native CEO and local team deliver training in the language your staff are most comfortable in — which is a major driver of adoption for Japanese-affiliated companies in the Philippines.
Is training onsite or online?
Either. We run hands-on sessions at your office in Metro Manila or remotely, scheduled around your team. Training uses your real workflows and data, not a generic demo, so what people learn maps directly to their daily work.
Can our team really maintain Kintone without coding?
Yes. Kintone is designed for non-technical users — Guhring Philippines had a single trained employee build 20+ apps with zero coding. Our training is built to make your team self-sufficient, not dependent on us. When you eventually want logic beyond no-code, we offer customization.
How much does Kintone training cost?
Training is priced by scope — by team size, the number of sessions, and the depth you need — so you get one fixed quote up front, never an hourly meter. Book a free consultation and we'll propose a plan and a fixed quote.
