The real risk

Most enterprise software projects in the Philippines fail. The only question is which way.

Decision-makers get squeezed from two sides. Stay too manual and the business can't scale. Over-buy a heavyweight ERP and you inherit an 18-month rollout you may never finish. There's a safer path between them.

Failure mode 1 — too little

Running on paper & Excel

  • Approvals chased over Viber, email, and group chats
  • Spreadsheets that break, conflict, and lose version history
  • No audit trail, no permissions, no single source of truth
